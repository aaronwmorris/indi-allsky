Solve matches stars in the displayed image using the camera location and
                           image time, which must be correct. It starts from the entered alignment and searches for
                           Altitude and Camera pointing direction if needed. Recovered pointing resets latitude/longitude offsets
                           to zero. Results update the fields and overlay for review; nothing is saved.

                        
Use a clear, star-rich frame from the Gallery. Clouds, bright sky and obstructions can
                           prevent a reliable match. A reasonable diameter and image-center estimate help the search.

                        
New solves account for the catalogue reference date. Save retains this correction;
                           existing saved alignments keep their original coordinates until solved again.

                        
Lens curvature separates radial lens distortion from camera tilt. It is calibrated by
                           the normal solver; zero retains the original equisolid projection.

                        
Save writes all nine calibration values to the camera configuration,
                           including manually entered values. Display controls are not saved. Azimuth also repositions
                           the cardinal-direction labels burned into future captured images.

                        
Additional overlay correction is optional and can reduce residual mismatches
                           after the normal solve. It requires a reliable star match and uses the detection mask
                           and sky ROI as hints. The correction is accepted only when independent validation stars
                           support an improvement; otherwise the normal alignment is kept and the reason is shown.
                           Save stores the model and whether it is enabled; only the overlay is corrected.

                        
Reload on Save also reloads the capture service to apply the saved
                           configuration. If unchecked, the values become page defaults after the next service reload.

                        
Direction labels stay inside the visible photo and its image mask. Directions outside
                           the camera's view are shown near the edge as direction cues.
