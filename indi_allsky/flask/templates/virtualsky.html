Solve detects stars in the image shown below and fits the six geometry fields above (all but Magnitude) to match the sky at that image's timestamp. Results fill the form for review — nothing is saved. The entered values seed the search: any Azimuth Angle works, the Diameter can be off by roughly 40%, but the X/Y Offsets must be within about 100 px of correct. Cloudy, moonlit, or twilight frames are refused — open a clear frame from the Gallery and solve that instead.

Save writes LENS_AZIMUTH and the VIRTUALSKY offset/diameter settings above to the camera configuration. Azimuth also repositions the cardinal-direction labels burned into future captured images.
