Solve matches stars in the displayed image using the camera location and image time, which must be correct. It starts from the entered alignment and searches for camera elevation and azimuth if needed. Recovered pointing resets latitude/longitude offsets to zero. Results update the fields and overlay for review; nothing is saved.

Use a clear, star-rich frame from the Gallery. Clouds, bright sky and obstructions can prevent a reliable match. A reasonable diameter and image-center estimate help the search.

Save writes all eight calibration values to the camera configuration, including manually entered values. Display controls are not saved. Rotation also repositions the cardinal-direction labels burned into future captured images.

Reload on Save also reloads the capture service to apply the saved configuration. If unchecked, the values become page defaults after the next service reload.

With a tilted camera, direction labels stay near their projected horizon positions; labels beyond the reference circle are placed at its rim as direction cues.
