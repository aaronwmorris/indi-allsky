Derive purple-frame repair values from FITS already saved by indi-allsky or from one manually selected collection.
How it works
Both input methods use the same classifier, adjacent-frame matching, validation, and background worker. Browser uploads remain private and are deleted as soon as calibration finishes.
Evidence needed
Calibration needs at least seven matched bad frames and seven distinct compatible normal frames, daylight highlights, and at least two exposure levels. Unusable files are reported and ignored.
Current calibration-evidence settings

These settings describe future captures; the tool will ultimately judge stored FITS by what actually exists, not by how it was saved. The recommended low-disk starting point is Exclude Only together with Bad and Following RAW FITS.
