Other actions








Force explicitly overrides ordinary quota limits, but still requires a Preview and a minute between publication attempts. Remove turns publication off and asks the map to delete this listing.











How publishing works





Automatic updates — While registered and enabled, with no suppression or review hold, the listing refreshes about every 48 hours and soon after any published detail changes: location or coordinates, Owner, Lens, Privacy Mode, these URLs, Equipment, detected camera or computer, or an INDI-AllSky upgrade that changes the published version.



Limits — The client allows up to 5 ordinary attempts in a rolling 24 hours, at least a minute apart. Failed or uncertain attempts count. Server update counts use the server’s own accounting window. Force bypasses the ordinary client cap and requests a server-limit override; the one-minute guard and single-flight protection still apply. Force is never automatic. Remove does not consume the ordinary local budget.



Turning publication off — Disabling stops future updates but leaves the listing on the map. Remove and disable also asks the map to delete it. Either way, publication stays paused until you Preview and Register again. An already-sent request cannot be recalled.



Uncertain results — If a request may or may not have reached the server, it is never retried automatically. Automatic updates pause until you review and Register again.



Privacy Mode — Privacy Mode only reduces coordinates to whole degrees in this listing. Owner, location name, URLs and images are still public as shown in Preview, and images or text can reveal where you are.



Location precision — Published coordinates are limited by the precision of your saved location. Rounding can change or eliminate the intended grid spacing. Choosing a coarser setting later does not remove locations that were already public.



Installation identifier — A random ID, created at first Preview, identifies this listing to the map. Keep it private. Anyone with it could change or remove your listing. It is not your host machine ID and is not used to log in to indi-allsky. It survives disable, re-enable and upgrades; it is kept in the database backup. No image bytes or full configuration are sent.
